/// Format & Rules

How the ladder actually works.

Check-in & no-shows+
Squads must check in through the WhatsApp group 30 minutes before their scheduled slot. Two missed check-ins in a season forfeit the match and any paid entry fee.
Scoring system+
PUBG Mobile and Delta Force Mobile use placement points plus 1 point per elimination. COD Mobile is straight series score — best of three until the semifinals, best of five in the final.
Anti-cheat & fair play+
Screen recording is required for all squads from the semifinals onward. Any use of emulator exploits, third-party aim tools, or teaming results in immediate disqualification and forfeiture of prize eligibility.
Refunds+
Entry fees are refunded in full if a bracket is cancelled before its first match. Once the bracket starts, fees are non-refundable regardless of match outcome.
