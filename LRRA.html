About Us

The Lahore Ring Road Authority (LRRA) is responsible for planning, development, maintenance, and regulation of the Lahore Ring Road. Our mission is to provide a safe and efficient transportation system for the people of Lahore.

Services

  • Traffic Management & Monitoring
  • Maintenance of Roads & Infrastructure
  • Public Safety & Awareness Campaigns
  • Toll Management System

Major Projects

LRRA has completed several development projects including interchanges, service areas, and road expansions to ease traffic flow in Lahore.
